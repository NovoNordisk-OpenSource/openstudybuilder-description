    OpenStudyBuilder¶

    OpenStudyBuilder is the open-source solution for the industry establishing a single, standardized source of truth for digital study design specifications unlocking data and AI-driven operational and scientific excellence across clinical development.

    Try the Sandbox Get Started

    See It in Action¶

    Why OpenStudyBuilder?¶

    Clinical study processes today are characterized by:

    • Silos and rework - parallel work across disconnected teams leads to discrepancies and delays
    • Manual, error-prone handovers - between professional groups, IT systems, and tools
    • No end-to-end consistency - terminology used in protocol, CRF, and datasets diverges, blocking automation

    OpenStudyBuilder solves this by implementing study specifications as linked, standards-driven metadata - one source of truth that feeds every downstream system.

    What It Enables¶

    End-to-end process support
    From protocol authoring and CRF design through clinical system setup to submission data - all driven by shared standards, templates, and study design metadata.

    One source of truth
    Central storage of study design data and standards for multiple downstream usages, with versioning and full audit trail.

    Automation and efficiency
    Reduces manual steps, minimises errors and discrepancies, and supports automated downstream processes - resulting in faster reporting and submissions.

    Modern, open architecture
    Graph database, transparent API-based design, and a modular implementation that supports partial adoption and easy integration with existing systems.

    Community-driven innovation
    Project released under the CDISC Open Source Alliance (COSA) built on CDISC 360i and Digital Data Flow (DDF) principles - open to contributions from the entire industry.

    Who Uses It¶

    OpenStudyBuilder supports cross-functional study teams across the full clinical development lifecycle:

    Standards Governance • Study Designer • Standards Management • Trial Management • Medical Writing • Data Management • Biostatistics • Medical & Science • Clinical Pharmacology

    Project Milestones¶

    • Q3 2022 - First open-source release
    • Q4 2023 - First business go-live at Novo Nordisk; all Phase 2/3 studies using OpenStudyBuilder for protocol authoring
    • Oct 2025 - Schedule of Activities live for all interventional studies (Phase 1–4) at Novo Nordisk
    • Dec 2025 - First external code contribution from a pharmaceutical company (Boehringer Ingelheim)
    • May 2026 - Signed collaboration agreement between Novo Nordisk, Bayer, and Boehringer Ingelheim - three major pharma companies jointly committing to the development and maintenance of OpenStudyBuilder
    • 2026 - Industry adoption year: EDC automation, broader community rollout
